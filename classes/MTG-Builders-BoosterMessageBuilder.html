BoosterMessageBuilder extends MessageBuilder
in package
  • Application

BoosterMessageBuilder.php : 41

An opened booster pack as a Components V2 message: every card with its rarity, foil and price, the pack's value, a picker to look at a card, and buttons to see the pack's images or open another.

Custom ids (Boosters module): pack:open:<set>:<type>, pack:images:<pack>, pack:card:<pack> (the picker, valued with a uuid), where <pack> is the opened pack's id in the search cache.
