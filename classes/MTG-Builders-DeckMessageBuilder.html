DeckMessageBuilder extends MessageBuilder
in package
  • Application

DeckMessageBuilder.php : 40

A preconstructed deck as a Components V2 message: its commander, the main board grouped by card type with counts, the sideboard, a picker to look at a card, and an Export button for a decklist that MTG Arena and most deck sites import.

Custom ids (Decks module): deck:export:<fileName> and deck:card (the picker, valued with a uuid).
