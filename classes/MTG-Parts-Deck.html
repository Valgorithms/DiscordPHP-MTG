Deck.php : 45

A preconstructed deck — MTGJSON's Deck model, or a Deck List entry (no cards) when it comes from {@see \MTG\Repository\DeckRepository::getDecks()}.

Cards in a deck are Cards with count (and isFoil/isEtched).
