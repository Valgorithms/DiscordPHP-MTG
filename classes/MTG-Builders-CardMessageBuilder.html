CardMessageBuilder extends MessageBuilder
in package
  • Application

CardMessageBuilder.php : 51

Components V2 messages about one card: the card itself — art, rules text, today's price, its set, and buttons for everything else — and the panels those buttons open (rulings, legality, prices, foreign names, images, JSON).

Buttons carry stable custom ids, routed by the Cards module: card:<action>:<uuid>[:<context>], where the context (page.<search>.<n> or random.<search>) is how the card was reached, kept so the view can offer its way back after a flip or a change of printing.
