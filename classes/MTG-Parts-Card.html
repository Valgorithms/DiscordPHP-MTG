Card.php : 136

One face of one printing of a Magic: The Gathering card — MTGJSON's Card (Set) model, plus the Card (Deck) fields when it comes from a deck.

Multi-face cards (split, transform, modal DFC, adventure, …) have one Card per face, sharing name and told apart by faceName and side.
