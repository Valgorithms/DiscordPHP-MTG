CardRepository extends AbstractRepository
in package
  • Application
uses DatabaseRepositoryTrait

CardRepository.php : 34

Card printings from MTGJSON's AllPrintings build — search with any {@see CardQuery} filter, and fetch one by its MTGJSON `uuid` — hydrating {@see Card} parts with their identifiers, legalities, rulings, foreign data and purchase URLs.

MTGJSON serves cards only inside bulk files, so these are answered from the local Database copy rather than over HTTP.
